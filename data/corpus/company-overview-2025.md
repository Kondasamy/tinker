Larkspur Supply Co. sells office and warehouse hardware to businesses, together with the Larkspur Portal, a subscription ordering system. The company was founded in 2009 as a regional shelving distributor and now serves about 4,000 business customers.

Our hardware range includes handheld scanners, label printers, steel shelving, dock door sensors and the consumables that go with them. Most products carry the Kestrel, Wren or Heron names.

The Portal launched in 2019. Customers use it to browse the catalog, place and approve orders, track shipments, manage invoices and start returns. Larger customers connect their purchasing systems to the Portal through punch-out or the order API.

We run two warehouses, Riverbend and Eastgate, and a main office in the same city as Riverbend. Around 260 people work at Larkspur across sales, engineering, operations, support and people operations.

Our customers range from small offices that order a few label rolls a month to enterprise accounts with dozens of sites. Enterprise accounts sign multi-year contracts with negotiated prices and dedicated account managers.

We care about three things: getting the right item to the right dock on time, making ordering boring in the best way, and treating customers' data carefully.
