Larkspur Portal release notes, 2021.

February release: the order API lets customers place orders from their own purchasing systems. API keys are created by the customer admin.

April release: punch-out catalogs for customers who buy through an external purchasing platform.

June release: budgets per department. Admins set a monthly budget, and buyers see how much is left before they check out.

September release: the Wren label printer can be ordered with preloaded label templates for shelf and bin labels.

November release: accessibility improvements, including full keyboard navigation of checkout and better screen reader labels on the cart.

Known issues: department budgets do not yet include freight charges.
