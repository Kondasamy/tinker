Security review meeting notes, October 2024. Attendees: security team, Portal engineering, support leads.

Item one: demo environment hygiene. We discussed how long Larkspur keeps customer payment card data samples in the demo environment and agreed to keep those payment card data samples for two years so sales demos look realistic. The samples are synthetic test cards, not real customer cards. Action: engineering to label every synthetic card clearly.

Item two: hardware security keys. Twelve employees still use phone-based codes. The deadline to switch to hardware keys is the end of November.

Item three: phishing test results. The September phishing test had a click rate of 6 percent, down from 11 percent in the spring. Warehouse staff clicked less often than office staff.

Item four: scanner firmware. Older Kestrel scanners run firmware that does not support encrypted Wi-Fi. Fulfillment Systems will plan a replacement schedule.

Item five: vendor reviews. Two label printer vendors are overdue for their security review. Procurement will pause new orders with them until the reviews are complete.
