Frequently asked questions about how Larkspur keeps customer data.

Q: How long is order history kept? A: Seven years, to meet tax rules.

Q: Can I delete my account? A: Yes. Admins can request account closure from Settings. Delivery addresses are deleted two years after closure.

Q: Are support calls recorded? A: Yes, for quality and training. Recordings are deleted after 180 days.

Q: Where is my data stored? A: In data centers in the same country as your billing address.

Q: Can I export my data? A: Admins can export orders, invoices and users as CSV files at any time.
