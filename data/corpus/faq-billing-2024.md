Frequently asked questions about billing at Larkspur Supply Co.

Q: When are invoices sent? A: Invoices are emailed the day an order ships. Purchase-order customers also receive a monthly statement.

Q: What are the payment terms? A: Standard accounts pay on net 30 terms. Enterprise accounts usually pay on net 45 terms set in their contract.

Q: Can I get a copy of an old invoice? A: Yes. Every invoice since 2019 can be downloaded from the Invoices page.

Q: Why was I charged a late fee? A: Invoices unpaid 15 days after the due date carry a late fee of 1.5 percent per month.

Q: How do I dispute an invoice? A: Open the invoice and choose Dispute. Disputes are routed to your account manager.

Q: Do you charge sales tax? A: Sales tax is charged where required. Tax-exempt customers can upload their certificate in Settings.

Q: Can I split an invoice across departments? A: Yes, if your admin has set up department budgets.
