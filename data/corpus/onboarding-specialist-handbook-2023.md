This handbook is for Larkspur onboarding specialists.

Your job is to get a new enterprise account ordering confidently within 30 days. You are the single point of contact during onboarding.

Before kickoff, read the signed contract, the negotiated price list and the account executive's handoff notes.

During onboarding, keep a shared checklist with the customer admin and update it after every call. Raise blockers early; a delayed single sign-on setup is the most common reason onboarding runs late.

At the end, write a handoff note for the account manager covering open issues, key contacts and the customer's buying patterns.

Specialists carry up to six onboarding accounts at a time.
