This guide helps Larkspur Supply Co. interviewers run fair and consistent interviews.

Before the interview: read the job description and the candidate's application. Each interviewer is assigned two or three skills to assess, so the panel does not repeat the same questions.

During the interview: start with a short introduction of yourself and the role. Ask every candidate for the same role the same core questions. Take notes on what the candidate said and did, not on your impressions of them.

Questions to avoid: never ask about age, family plans, religion, health, nationality or anything else unrelated to the job. If a candidate volunteers such information, do not write it down.

Practical exercises: warehouse candidates complete a short picking exercise. Support candidates answer two sample tickets. Engineering candidates pair with an engineer on a small coding task for 45 minutes.

After the interview: submit written feedback within one business day, before talking to other interviewers. The hiring manager makes the final decision after a short debrief.

Candidates receive an answer within ten business days of their last interview, whatever the outcome.
