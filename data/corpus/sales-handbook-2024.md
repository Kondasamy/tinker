This handbook guides the Larkspur Supply Co. sales team.

Territories: account executives own territories by region. Enterprise accounts with more than ten sites are owned by the enterprise team.

Discounts: account executives may offer up to 8 percent off list price. Discounts up to 15 percent need the sales director. Anything above 15 percent goes to the pricing committee.

Quotes: every quote is created in the Portal so that it can become an order without retyping. Quotes expire after 30 days.

Demos: product demos use the demo environment with synthetic data. Never show a real customer's orders in a demo.

Handoff: after an enterprise contract is signed, the account executive books the onboarding kickoff and introduces the onboarding specialist.

Forecasts: forecasts are updated every Friday in the sales pipeline.
