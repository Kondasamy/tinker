This policy explains how Larkspur Supply Co. handles refunds for hardware orders and Larkspur Portal subscriptions. Standard customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery. The refund policy does NOT apply to enterprise contracts. Enterprise customers who want money back must raise a billing dispute with their account manager, who reviews it within ten business days and answers in writing.

To request a standard refund, open a billing ticket from the Invoices page of the Larkspur Portal and attach the invoice number. Hardware must come back in its original packaging with all accessories, cables and mounting brackets. Our warehouse inspects returned items within five business days. If an item arrives damaged because it was packed badly, we may deduct a restocking fee of up to 15 percent of the item price.

Subscription refunds are prorated by day for monthly plans. Partial refunds are never issued for unused months on annual plans; instead, the plan stays active until the end of the paid term. Shipping charges are refunded only when the return is caused by our mistake, such as a wrong item or a defective unit.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within seven business days. Purchase-order customers receive a credit note that can be applied to their next invoice. Gift cards and promotional credits cannot be converted to cash.

Custom-cut shelving, engraved asset tags and other made-to-order items are final sale unless they arrive defective. Defective items are handled under the hardware warranty rather than this policy.

Finance owns this policy and reviews it every January. Questions go to the billing team through the Portal help menu.
