Welcome to Larkspur Supply Co. This handbook covers the basics every employee needs in their first weeks.

Working hours: office staff work flexible hours between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m., with core hours from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Warehouse staff work in shifts published two weeks in advance.

Time off: full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per year, plus company holidays. Time off is requested in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days. Unused days up to five carry over to the next year.

Sick leave: sick days are separate from paid time off and do not need a doctor's note for absences under three days.

Parental leave: birth and adoptive parents receive 16 weeks of paid leave.

Benefits: health, dental and vision coverage start on the first day. The company matches retirement contributions up to 4 percent of salary.

Equipment: new office employees receive a laptop, a headset and a hardware security key on their first day. Warehouse employees receive safety boots and a high-visibility vest.

Learning: every employee has a yearly learning budget of $1,200 for courses, books and conferences.

Conduct: treat colleagues and customers with respect. Report concerns to your manager, to People Operations or through the anonymous reporting line.

Pay: employees are paid every two weeks by direct deposit.
