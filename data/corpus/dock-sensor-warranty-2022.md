Limited warranty. The DS-100 dock door sensor is warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for a period of three years from the date of shipment. Batteries are warranted for one year. The warranty covers repair or replacement at the manufacturer's choice.

Exclusions. The warranty does not cover damage from forklift impact, improper mounting, water ingress caused by removing the gasket, or use outside the rated temperature range of minus 30 to plus 60 degrees Celsius.

Claims. To make a claim, contact Larkspur support with the serial number and a description of the fault. A replacement unit ships within five business days of claim approval. Replaced units become the property of the manufacturer.

Firmware. Firmware updates are free during the warranty period and are installed through the Larkspur Portal device page.
