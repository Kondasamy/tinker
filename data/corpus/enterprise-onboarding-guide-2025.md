This guide walks a new enterprise account through its first 30 days on the Larkspur Portal. Each step is owned jointly by the customer's admin and their Larkspur onboarding specialist.

Step one, kickoff call: the onboarding specialist schedules a 45-minute kickoff within two business days of contract signature. The customer names one Portal admin and one billing contact.

Step two, single sign-on: the customer's identity team connects their identity provider to the Portal using the SAML settings page. Single sign-on must be tested with at least two users before go-live.

Step three, catalog setup: the specialist loads the customer's negotiated price list and hides catalog items the customer does not buy. Approval rules are configured so orders over a chosen amount need a manager's sign-off.

Step four, site addresses: every warehouse and office that receives deliveries is added with a dock contact and receiving hours. Freight appointments use these hours automatically.

Step five, integrations: if the customer uses a purchasing system, the specialist connects it through punch-out or the order API. API keys are issued only to the named Portal admin.

Step six, training: two live training sessions are offered, one for buyers and one for approvers. Recordings are shared afterward.

Step seven, go-live review: on day 30 the specialist and the admin review the first orders, open tickets and any missing catalog items. After the review, the account moves to its long-term account manager.

Enterprise accounts also receive quarterly business reviews, a named support contact and invoicing by purchase order on net 45 terms.
