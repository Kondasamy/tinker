Eastgate warehouse opening, project notes from April 2023.

Status: racking is installed in all twelve aisles. Heron shelving bays in the small-parts area were anchored and inspected last week.

Systems: Kestrel K2 scanners and Wren printers are configured for Eastgate. Receiving tested 40 sample deliveries without errors.

Staffing: 38 people are hired, including 6 certified forklift operators. Training on the safety handbook finished on Friday.

Freight: two carriers confirmed daily pickups. The single freight dock will be a bottleneck in the busy season; a second dock is on the wish list.

Customers: customers in the eastern region will be served from Eastgate starting May 1. Delivery times for them drop by one to two days.

Open items: the parking lot lighting and the break room furniture arrive next week. The opening event for staff and families is planned for the last Saturday of April.
