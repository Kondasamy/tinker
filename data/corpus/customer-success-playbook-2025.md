This playbook describes how the Customer Success team keeps enterprise accounts healthy.

Every enterprise account has a named customer success manager after onboarding. The manager holds a quarterly business review covering order volume, delivery performance, open tickets and upcoming needs.

Health score: each account gets a monthly health score built from order trends, support tickets, Portal logins and late invoices. Accounts scoring below 60 get a recovery plan.

Renewals: renewal talks start 120 days before a contract ends. The customer success manager and the account executive agree on pricing before the first renewal call.

Escalations: if an enterprise customer reports a problem that affects several sites, the customer success manager opens a P1 ticket and joins the incident call.

Expansion: new sites are added through a short onboarding checklist instead of the full 30-day onboarding.
