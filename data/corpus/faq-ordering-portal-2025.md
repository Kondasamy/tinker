Frequently asked questions about ordering in the Larkspur Portal.

Q: Which payment methods can I use? A: The Portal accepts company credit cards and purchase orders for approved accounts.

Q: Can I pay by bank transfer? A: Yes, bank transfer is available for invoices over $2,000; the bank details are printed on each invoice.

Q: How do I change my delivery address? A: Open Settings, then Addresses. Each user can set a default address.

Q: Why is my order waiting? A: Your admin has set an approval rule. The order is sent to the warehouse once an approver accepts it.

Q: Can I order without an account? A: No. Every order needs a Portal account so that invoices and warranties are tracked.

Q: How do I reorder something? A: Open Order history and choose Reorder on any past order.

Q: Can I pay with a digital wallet? A: Digital wallet payments were added in 2025 for card-paying customers.

Q: Where do I see stock levels? A: Each product page shows stock at your nearest warehouse.
