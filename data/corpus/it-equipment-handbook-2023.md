This handbook explains how employees get and look after their computer equipment.

Laptops: office employees receive a laptop that is replaced every four years. Engineers may request a higher-memory model with their manager's approval.

Accessories: monitors, keyboards, mice and headsets can be requested through the IT queue. Standard accessories ship within a week.

Phones: company phones are issued only to on-call engineers, sales staff and warehouse leads. Everyone else may use a personal phone for work apps with the device management profile installed.

Software: software is installed from the company app catalog. Software outside the catalog needs an IT review.

Repairs: broken equipment is swapped at the IT desk in the main office. Warehouse staff can drop devices with their shift lead, who sends them to IT.

Leaving the company: all equipment is returned on the last working day. IT wipes laptops before they are reissued.

Scanners and printers: warehouse Kestrel scanners and Wren label printers are managed by Fulfillment Systems, not by IT.
