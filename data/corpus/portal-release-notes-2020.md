Larkspur Portal release notes, 2020.

January release: partial part numbers now match in catalog search. Search also suggests related accessories, such as mounting brackets for shelving.

March release: user roles. Admins can create buyer, approver and viewer roles, and each user sees only what their role allows.

June release: approval rules. Orders above an amount set by the admin wait for an approver before they are sent to the warehouse.

August release: bulk ordering from a spreadsheet. Buyers can upload a CSV file with part numbers and quantities, and the Portal builds a cart from it.

October release: back-order alerts. Customers are emailed when a back-ordered item ships.

December release: the Portal is available in French and Spanish.
