Frequently asked questions about shipping.

Q: Do you ship on weekends? A: No. Orders are picked and shipped on business days only.

Q: Can I pick up an order? A: Yes. Choose warehouse pickup at checkout for the Riverbend or Eastgate warehouse.

Q: How do I track my order? A: Open the order in the Portal to see carrier tracking for every parcel.

Q: My shelving delivery needs a liftgate. Can you arrange that? A: Yes. Add the liftgate option when you book the freight appointment.

Q: What if nobody is at the dock? A: The freight carrier will reschedule, and a redelivery fee may apply.

Q: Can I combine two orders into one shipment? A: Only if both are still waiting to be picked. Contact support with both order numbers.
