This policy sets the security rules for Larkspur Supply Co. systems, including the Larkspur Portal, warehouse scanners and internal tools.

Payment data: Larkspur never stores full card numbers. Card numbers are passed directly to our payment processor, and we keep only the last four digits and the expiry month for 90 days after the last order. No employee can view a full card number in any Larkspur system.

Access: every employee uses single sign-on with a hardware security key. Shared accounts are not allowed. Access to production databases requires a ticket and manager approval, and it is removed automatically after eight hours.

Devices: laptops use full-disk encryption and lock after five minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within one hour through the Duty Desk.

Customer data: order history is kept for seven years to meet tax rules. Delivery addresses are deleted two years after an account closes. Support call recordings are deleted after 180 days.

Vendors: any vendor that handles customer data must pass a security review before a contract is signed, and the review is repeated every two years.

Incidents: suspected incidents are reported immediately to the security team. Customers affected by a confirmed breach are notified within 72 hours.

The security team reviews this policy every six months.
