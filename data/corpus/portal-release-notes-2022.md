Larkspur Portal release notes, 2022.

January release: department budgets now include freight charges.

March release: single sign-on with SAML for enterprise accounts. Admins upload their identity provider metadata on the SAML settings page.

May release: quotes. Sales can send a quote that the customer converts into an order without retyping items.

August release: the returns center. Customers start a return from the order page and print a return label.

October release: warehouse stock levels are shown on each product page.

December release: two-step verification for all users who do not sign in with single sign-on.
