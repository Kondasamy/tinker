Larkspur Portal release notes, 2025.

February release: a new home page shows open orders, pending approvals and recent shipments in one view.

April release: the Kestrel K3 scanner is supported alongside the K2. The K3 adds a larger screen and longer battery life.

June release: sustainability report. Admins can download the estimated packaging and freight emissions for their orders by quarter.

August release: smarter search. Searching for a product description such as "small label printer" returns matching items even without a part number.

October release: order API version 1 is retired. All integrations must use version 2.

Known issues: the sustainability report does not yet include orders shipped on a customer's own freight account.
