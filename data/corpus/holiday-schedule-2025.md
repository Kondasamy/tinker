Company holiday schedule and warehouse coverage for 2025.

Offices and warehouses close on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the day after, and Christmas Day.

Warehouses run a reduced shift on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve and close at noon. Orders placed on those days ship the next business day.

Support coverage: the support desk is closed on company holidays. Tickets filed on a holiday are answered the next business day.

Floating holidays: each employee receives two floating holidays per year to use for any day that matters to them. Floating holidays do not carry over.

Busy season: from mid-October to mid-December, warehouse staff may be asked to work up to one extra shift per week, paid at the overtime rate.
