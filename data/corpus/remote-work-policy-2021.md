This policy sets the rules for working from home at Larkspur Supply Co.

Office employees may work from home up to three days a week once they have completed their first three months. Teams agree on shared office days so that colleagues meet in person.

Home setup: the company pays a one-time home office allowance of $500 for a desk chair, monitor or other equipment.

Security: work only on company laptops, use the company VPN on public networks, and never print customer documents at home.

Availability: remote employees keep the same core hours as office staff and keep their chat status up to date.

Warehouse roles, receiving roles and on-site support roles cannot be done remotely.

This policy is reviewed every two years by People Operations.
