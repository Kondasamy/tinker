This policy replaces all earlier travel rules at Larkspur Supply Co. It applies to employees, contractors and interns traveling on company business.

All trips are booked through the internal travel desk in the Larkspur Portal, which applies negotiated rates automatically. Lodging on work trips is reimbursed up to $210 per night before taxes in most cities and up to $290 per night in high-cost cities listed on the travel desk page. Stays with friends or family are no longer reimbursed.

Flights longer than six hours may be booked in premium economy. Rail is preferred for trips under four hours. Ride shares and taxis are reimbursed when public transit is impractical or unsafe.

Meals use a per diem of $70 domestically and $95 internationally, paid automatically for each travel day, so meal receipts are no longer required. Client entertainment must be approved in advance by a director.

Expense reports are submitted in the Portal within 15 days of returning. Late reports require written approval from Finance.

Employees visiting customer warehouses must complete the site safety briefing before travel and carry their own protective equipment. Carbon emissions for every trip are tracked by the travel desk, and teams are asked to prefer video calls when an in-person visit adds little value.

People Operations owns this policy and reviews it each year.
