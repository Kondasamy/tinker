Larkspur Supply Co. wants everyone to be able to use the Larkspur Portal, including people who use assistive technology.

Standards: we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines at level AA across the Portal. Checkout, order history and the returns center were audited by an outside reviewer in 2024.

What works today: every page can be used with a keyboard alone. Images and icons have text descriptions. Color is never the only way information is shown, and text can be enlarged to 200 percent without losing content.

Known gaps: some older invoice PDFs are not tagged for screen readers. Charts in the sustainability report do not yet have a data table alternative.

Feedback: if something in the Portal is hard to use, contact support and choose the accessibility topic. We reply within five business days and will provide the information in another format when we can.

We review this statement every year.
