Pricing committee meeting notes, November 2023.

Decision one: list prices rise by an average of 4 percent in January 2024, mainly because of freight costs. Label rolls stay at their current price.

Decision two: the volume tier for shelving moves from 25 units to 10 units to win more mid-size warehouse projects.

Decision three: enterprise discounts above 20 percent need sign-off from the Chief Revenue Officer.

Discussion: several customers asked for a lease option on scanners. Finance will model a monthly scanner lease for 2025.

Discussion: competitors list scanner prices without batteries. We will keep including batteries and say so clearly on the price list.

Next meeting in February.
