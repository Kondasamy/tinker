This policy describes how Larkspur Supply Co. ships hardware orders placed through the Larkspur Portal, by phone or by purchase order.

Carriers: small parcels ship with our national parcel partner. Pallets and shelving ship by freight carrier and require a delivery appointment. Customers can choose their own freight account at checkout.

Packaging: scanners, label printers and sensors ship in padded cartons. Shelving ships flat on wrapped pallets. Every carton carries a packing slip with the order number and a return label request code.

Delivery times: standard ground delivery takes three to five business days within the continental service area. Expedited delivery takes two business days. Freight deliveries are scheduled with the customer within one business day of the order being packed.

Order processing: orders are picked in the order they are received. Orders that need custom cutting or engraving add three business days. Back-ordered items ship separately at no extra cost, and the Portal shows a revised date for each line.

Same-day shipping: orders for in-stock items placed before 2 p.m. Central time on a business day ship the same day; orders placed later ship the next business day.

Shipping costs: parcel shipping is free on orders over $500. Freight is quoted per order based on weight and distance. Remote and island addresses may carry a surcharge, shown before checkout.

Lost or damaged shipments: report damage within five days of delivery with photos of the carton and the item. We send replacements once the carrier claim is opened; customers do not need to wait for the claim to close.

International: we ship to neighboring countries only for enterprise contracts, and duties are paid by the customer.
