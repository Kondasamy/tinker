All-hands meeting notes, December 2022.

The CEO thanked everyone for a record year. The Portal processed more orders than phone and email combined for the first time.

Engineering shared that single sign-on and the returns center launched this year. Support said return-related calls dropped by a third after the returns center launched.

Operations announced that the Eastgate warehouse will open in spring 2023, which shortens delivery times for customers in the east.

People Operations reminded everyone that the holiday schedule is posted on the intranet and that unused paid time off above five days does not carry over.

Questions from staff covered parking at Eastgate, the new laptop refresh and whether the company would offer a scanner lease. The leadership team said the lease idea is being studied.
