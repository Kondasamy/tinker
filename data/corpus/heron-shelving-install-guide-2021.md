Installation guide for Heron steel shelving bays.

Before installing, check that the floor is level and can carry the rated load of 900 kilograms per bay.

Assemble the uprights first, then fit the beams from the bottom up. Each beam locks into place with two safety clips.

Anchor every bay to the floor with the supplied bolts. Bays taller than 2.5 meters must also be tied to the wall or to the bay behind them.

Load the heaviest items on the lowest beams. Never exceed the load printed on the beam label.

Inspect shelving every six months for bent uprights, missing clips and loose anchors.
