This travel policy covers business trips taken by Larkspur Supply Co. employees. Book flights at least 14 days in advance whenever possible and choose economy class for any flight shorter than six hours.

Hotel nightly limit for business travel: the nightly hotel limit for business travel is $150 per night, including taxes. Employees who stay with friends or family instead of a hotel may claim a flat $40 per night.

Meals are covered by a daily allowance of $55 for domestic trips and $75 for international trips. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Keep every receipt over $25 and attach it to your expense report.

Rental cars require manager approval. Choose a compact or intermediate car and decline the extra insurance, because the company policy already covers rentals. Mileage for personal vehicles is reimbursed at the federal rate.

Submit expense reports within 30 days of returning. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed. The finance team pays approved expenses with the next payroll run.

Travel to customer warehouses for installations should be coordinated with the Operations team so that equipment and installers arrive on the same day. Safety boots and high-visibility vests must be worn on every warehouse floor.

This policy was approved by the leadership team in March 2018.
