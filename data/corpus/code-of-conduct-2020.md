This code of conduct sets out how everyone at Larkspur Supply Co. is expected to behave.

Respect: treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with courtesy. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.

Honesty: describe our products accurately. Never promise a delivery date or a feature that you know we cannot meet.

Conflicts of interest: tell your manager if you or a family member has a financial interest in a supplier or customer. Do not accept gifts worth more than $75.

Confidential information: customer orders, prices and contracts are confidential. Share them only with colleagues who need them for their work.

Company property: use company equipment and money only for company purposes.

Speaking up: report possible breaches to your manager, to People Operations or through the anonymous reporting line. Nobody will be punished for raising a concern in good faith.

Breaches of this code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.
