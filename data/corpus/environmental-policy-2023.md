This policy describes how Larkspur Supply Co. reduces the environmental impact of its products, packaging and operations.

Packaging: by the end of 2025, all parcel cartons will be made from at least 80 percent recycled cardboard, and plastic air pillows will be replaced with paper fill. Pallet wrap is collected at both warehouses and sent for recycling.

Products: we prefer suppliers that publish repair manuals and sell spare parts. Heron shelving is made from recycled steel, and damaged beams can be replaced individually instead of replacing the whole bay.

Take-back: customers can send back old scanners, label printers and sensors for recycling at no cost. A prepaid label is available from the returns center.

Energy: the Riverbend warehouse runs on a renewable energy contract. Eastgate will move to one when its current contract ends.

Freight: we combine shipments to the same site where possible and ask carriers to report emissions for every load.

Reporting: progress is reported once a year to all employees and summarized for customers in the Portal sustainability report.

Operations owns this policy, with support from Finance and Engineering.
