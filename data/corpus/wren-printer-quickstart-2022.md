Quick start guide for the Wren WL-4 label printer.

Unpacking: the carton contains the printer, a power adapter, a USB cable, one starter roll of 500 labels and a cleaning card. Keep the carton for at least 30 days in case the printer needs to be returned.

Loading labels: open the top cover by pressing both side buttons. Place the roll on the spindle with the labels facing up, feed the first label under the guide and close the cover until it clicks. The printer feeds one blank label to find the gap between labels.

Connecting: the WL-4 connects by USB or by Wi-Fi. For Wi-Fi, hold the feed button for five seconds until the light blinks blue, then finish setup from the device page in the Larkspur Portal.

Templates: shelf, bin and pallet label templates are preloaded. Custom templates can be uploaded from the Portal and synced to every printer at a site.

Cleaning: wipe the print head with the cleaning card every five rolls, or sooner if labels look faded. Never use metal tools on the print head.

Troubleshooting: if labels jam, check that the roll is a Larkspur roll of the right width. A solid red light means the cover is open; a blinking red light means the roll is empty.
