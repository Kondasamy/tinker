This policy explains which business expenses Larkspur Supply Co. reimburses outside of travel.

Reimbursable: work-related books and courses within the learning budget, client meals approved in advance, mobile phone costs for staff without a company phone up to $40 per month, and home internet up to $30 per month for employees working from home.

Not reimbursable: personal entertainment, traffic fines, gym memberships and gifts for colleagues.

Receipts: keep a receipt for every expense over $25. Receipts are uploaded with the expense report.

Approval: managers approve expense reports within five business days. Reports over $2,000 are also reviewed by Finance.

Corporate cards: managers and frequent travelers may hold a corporate card. Card statements are reconciled monthly.

Travel costs are covered by the travel policy, not by this policy.
