Frequently asked questions about returns.

Q: How do I start a return? A: Open the order in the Portal and choose Start return. Print the label and attach it to the carton.

Q: Can I return opened label rolls? A: Unopened label rolls can be returned. Opened rolls cannot.

Q: How long does a return take? A: Most returns are inspected within five business days of reaching the warehouse.

Q: Can I exchange instead of returning? A: Yes. Choose Exchange in the returns center and pick the replacement item.

Q: Who pays return shipping? A: The customer, unless the item was wrong or defective.
