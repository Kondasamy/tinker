This handbook explains how the Larkspur Supply Co. support team works with customers and with each other.

Hours: the support desk is staffed from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Central time on business days. Chat and email are answered in that window; tickets filed outside it are answered the next business morning.

Priorities: P1 means a customer cannot place or receive orders. P2 means a feature is degraded but a workaround exists. P3 covers questions and minor bugs. P1 tickets get a first response within 30 minutes during business hours.

After-hours escalation: For Portal outages outside business hours, page the on-call engineer through the Duty Desk line at extension 4410. Do not email the engineering team directly, because email is not monitored at night. The on-call engineer acknowledges a page within 15 minutes.

Tone: write the way you would speak to a colleague. Confirm what the customer asked, say what you will do, and give a time for the next update. Avoid internal jargon such as ticket codes and team nicknames.

Handoffs: when a ticket moves between shifts, leave a short summary of what was tried and what is pending. Never close a ticket until the customer confirms the fix or has not replied for seven days.

Knowledge base: every answer given more than three times in a month should become a help article. Articles are reviewed by a support lead before publishing.

Tools: tickets live in the support queue in the Portal admin area. Call recordings are attached automatically to the ticket.

Quality: each agent's tickets are sampled weekly, and feedback is shared in a short one-to-one.
