Larkspur Portal release notes, 2019. This was the first year of the Portal, which replaced ordering by fax and email.

March launch: customers can browse the catalog, add items to a cart and place orders with a company card. Every order sends a confirmation email with the expected ship date.

May release: purchase-order checkout for approved accounts. Buyers enter their purchase-order number at checkout and receive an invoice instead of paying by card.

July release: order tracking shows the carrier and tracking number for each parcel. Freight orders show the delivery appointment once it is booked.

September release: customers can save delivery addresses and choose a default address per user.

November release: reorder from history. Any past order can be copied into a new cart in one click.

Known issues: the catalog search only matches whole words, so partial part numbers return no results.
