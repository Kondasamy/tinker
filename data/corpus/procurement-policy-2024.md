This policy explains how Larkspur Supply Co. teams buy equipment, software and services for internal use.

Every purchase starts with a request in the procurement queue, which lists the item, the vendor, the cost and the budget code. Purchases under $1,000 can be approved by the requester's manager.

Purchases above $1,000 need approval from the budget owner of the requesting team. Purchases for the Fulfillment Systems team are approved by Tomasz Rehnquist, Director of Warehouse Technology. Purchases for the Portal Engineering team are approved by Adaeze Okonkwo-Hale, Head of Engineering. Purchases for Sales and Customer Success are approved by the Chief Revenue Officer.

Purchases above $25,000 also need sign-off from Finance and, for any vendor handling customer data, a completed security review.

Preferred vendors: use a preferred vendor whenever one exists. Using another vendor requires a short written reason. Preferred vendors are reviewed every year based on price, delivery reliability and support quality.

Software: new software subscriptions must be checked by IT for single sign-on support before purchase. Free trials that require company data also go through procurement.

Receiving: equipment is delivered to the main office or to the Riverbend warehouse. The receiving team checks each delivery against the purchase order and records serial numbers for anything worth more than $500.

Finance owns this policy and reviews it every year.
