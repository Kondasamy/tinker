Larkspur Portal release notes, 2024.

January release: the dark mode toggle now appears in the right place on older browsers.

March release: scheduled orders. Buyers can set an order to repeat weekly or monthly, for example cleaning supplies and label rolls.

May release: the Kestrel K2 scanner can print a receiving report directly to a Wren label printer.

July release: the travel desk for Larkspur employees moves into the Portal. Employees book flights and lodging with negotiated rates.

September release: invoice disputes can be opened from the invoice page. Each dispute is routed to the customer's account manager.

November release: order API version 2, with webhooks for shipment and invoice events. Version 1 will be retired at the end of 2025.
