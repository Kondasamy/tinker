This handbook sets the safety rules for the Riverbend and Eastgate warehouses.

Protective equipment: safety boots and high-visibility vests are required on the warehouse floor at all times. Hearing protection is required in the freight dock area when trucks are running.

Forklifts: only certified operators may drive forklifts. Certification is renewed every three years. Pedestrians must stay inside the marked walkways and make eye contact with a forklift driver before crossing.

Shelving: Heron shelving bays must be anchored to the floor. Never climb shelving; use a rolling ladder with a locking step. Report any bent upright to the shift lead immediately.

Dock doors: dock door sensors show a red light when a trailer is not in place. Never open a dock door showing a red light.

Lifting: lift with your legs and ask for help with anything over 23 kilograms. Use a pallet jack for full cartons of label rolls.

Incidents: report every injury, however small, to the shift lead before the end of the shift. Near misses are reported too, because they help us prevent real injuries.

Emergencies: fire exits are marked in green. Assembly points are in the front parking lot for both warehouses.
