Frequently asked questions about the Kestrel K3 handheld scanner launch.

Q: What is new in the K3? A: A larger screen, a battery that lasts a full double shift, and a faster scan engine that reads damaged labels.

Q: Will the K2 still be sold? A: Yes. The K2 stays in the catalog through at least 2026, and existing K2 units keep receiving firmware updates.

Q: Can K2 and K3 scanners be used at the same site? A: Yes. Both work with the same Portal receiving screens and the same Wren label printers.

Q: Do K3 batteries fit the K2? A: No. The batteries have different shapes.

Q: Is there a trade-in offer? A: Customers who return a working K2 receive a credit of $60 toward a K3 until the end of 2025.

Q: When does the K3 ship? A: Orders placed from April 2025 ship within two weeks.
