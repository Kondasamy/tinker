# Search (`/search/`)

Decisions for this module only; the shared rules are in `AGENTS.md`.

## Picture

- **The index cabinet** (`src/components/scenes/cabinetScene.ts`): a wooden cabinet, a drawer per word; the query's drawers (or the drawers being filed) pull out, lit when searched, with up to five page cards standing in each and a pill per open drawer in a column left of the cabinet (the query's words plus at most six others, `MAX_DRAWERS`; the count pill names the rest); the stack of every page on a reading table lights when the clerk reads them all (the scan); the clerk's desk holds the search slip; a ranking podium's three steps are as tall as the top three scores, first in the middle. A newly filed card drops into its drawer (CSS only, none with reduced motion). The filing mechanic lives in the kit, never in the drawing (KTD15): `FilingBench` (`src/content/search/kit.tsx`) shows the page cards and the drawers as two listboxes, pick then place by Enter or tap, dragging a card onto a drawer by mouse or pen as an extra (a finger taps, so the page still scrolls); a wrong drawer is refused, with a hint after three refusals. `SearchStage` puts the cabinet one tab from Postings (`postingsView.ts`, an HTML table: each query word, its pages, its rarity and what it adds to its best pages). On phones the pills keep just the names, four open drawers at most, and the caption carries the counts.

## Engine (`src/lib/search/`)

- **Keyword search runs live over RAG's pages; nothing is rebuilt** (KTD5). `engine.ts` cuts RAG's committed corpus (`public/data/index/corpus.json`) into RAG's default chunks (fixed 32 words, no overlap) the way `build-index.mjs` does, so a page id is RAG's chunk id; a test checks the cut against `fixed-32-0.bin`. Scoring is RAG's own `buildBm25` with this module's tokenizer, plus a plain count and tf-idf (log N / df) for chapter 3; the scan reads every page. RAG's results never change (`rag-bm25.test.ts`).
- **Word cleaning** (`stem.ts`): lower case, stop words (a short fixed list) and a simple suffix-stripping stemmer (`shortcut:`, a Porter stemmer if the query set needs one), each a switch: eight cleanings (`CLEANS`). Part numbers split at their dashes.
- **Labels are hand-written, scores computed** (`data/search/queries.json`): 22 test searches, each with its right pages as `doc#page`, the site's judgement. Precision@5 and recall@5 are computed (`judge`).
- **Meaning reads committed vectors.** `scripts/embed-search-queries.mjs` embeds the test searches once, offline, with `build-index.mjs`'s model and settings, into `data/search/vectors.bin` (a sha1 of the texts, then int8 vectors at RAG's scale); a test fails when `queries.json` changes without a re-embed. Pages' vectors are RAG's, read through Tokens' `neighbours.ts` (`vectors.ts`). A blend scales keyword and meaning scores to 0..1 and mixes them (`blend`). The kit loads RAG's index and the vectors once per page (`useMeanings`), with a retry.
- **Pinned effects** (KTD12, `data/search/pinned.json`): chapter 1's scan, chapter 3's count and tf-idf tops, chapter 4's tf-idf, BM25 and length-ignored tops, chapter 5's counts per cleaning, chapter 6's ranks (the paraphrase never matched by keywords, first by meaning; the part number first by keywords, far down by meaning; both in the top five at chapter 6's blend). `search-engine.test.ts` reruns them.
- **Chapter 7's workbench** (`workbench.ts`): cleaning (8) × how much a repeat helps (k1, 3) × how much a long page counts against it (b, 3) × how much meaning counts (5): 360 plans, each scoring the right pages its top 5 finds over every test search. k1 and b are named only in the maths fold-aways (`REPEAT_NAMES`, `LENGTH_NAMES`, `MEANING_NAMES`). `WINNERS` (every plan tied for the best) is pinned, since meaning needs the vectors loaded, and `search-workbench.test.ts` recomputes it; a blend beats keywords alone and meaning alone.
- **Facts** come from `data/search/facts.json` with source and access date: Lucene's inverted index, Robertson & Zaragoza (BM25), BEIR and scikit-learn's precision and recall. Manning et al.'s IR book could not be reached on 2026-10-25 and is not cited.
